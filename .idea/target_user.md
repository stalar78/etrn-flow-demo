# Target User

## Primary customer profile

A small or medium company with:

- recurring road freight shipments;
- dispatcher, logistics or warehouse staff;
- 1C, spreadsheets or mixed internal tools;
- no dedicated product team;
- need for a clearer workflow around electronic transport documents.

## Primary demo persona

Head of logistics who wants to see:

- current shipments;
- who must act next;
- where a document is delayed;
- whether a custom system can fit existing processes.

## Secondary personas

- owner;
- operations director;
- IT manager;
- chief accountant;
- 1C integrator.
