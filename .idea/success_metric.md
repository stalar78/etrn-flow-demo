# Success Metric

## Primary product metric

A first-time visitor can complete the core demo flow in 3–5 minutes without external instruction.

## Primary commercial metric

At least one qualified inquiry after the product page and targeted advertising are launched.

## Funnel diagnostics

- product page → demo open;
- demo open → guest entry;
- guest entry → shipment opened;
- shipment opened → role switch or action;
- action → problem scenario opened;
- demo activity → StalarVision CTA;
- CTA → qualified form submission or direct contact.

No conversion rate is promised before real traffic data exists.
