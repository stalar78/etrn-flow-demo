# Monetization

## Initial model

The demo itself is free and works as a commercial proof.

Revenue comes from services:

1. Readiness and process audit.
2. Integration design.
3. Custom dispatcher or logistics workspace.
4. Integration with 1C, CRM, ERP or an EPD operator.
5. Support and further development.

## Possible offer levels

### Audit

- current process mapping;
- participants and systems;
- gap analysis;
- implementation plan;
- preliminary estimate.

### Integration

- data exchange;
- statuses and webhooks;
- error handling;
- notifications;
- reconciliation.

### Individual application

- custom roles and workflows;
- mobile driver flow;
- dashboards;
- customer-specific integrations;
- support.

## Future product model

Only after repeated customer patterns are confirmed:

- setup fee;
- monthly support or subscription;
- optional per-organization or per-driver pricing;
- paid custom integrations.

The project must not prematurely promise a universal SaaS tariff.
