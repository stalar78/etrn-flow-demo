## Research rule

Before implementing or publishing claims about:

- effective dates;
- mandatory participants;
- document formats;
- accredited operators;
- API availability;
- signature requirements;
- exceptions;

verify them using current official legislation, regulator materials and official operator documentation.

Do not copy another operator's interface or brand style.
