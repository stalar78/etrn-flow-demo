# Product

## What we are building

A public interactive demonstration of a custom logistics web application around ETRN workflows.

It combines:

- shipment management;
- multiple operational roles;
- workflow statuses;
- event history;
- attention and delay control;
- future integration boundary.

## Why it matters

An accredited operator solves legally significant exchange. A customer's employees still need a convenient interface connected to their actual processes, systems and responsibilities.

The demo proves that StalarVision can build that interface.

## Current phase

Product definition and documentation.

## Out of scope

Real operator connection, GIS EPD, signatures, real documents, real customer data, 1C integration, billing and full TMS.
