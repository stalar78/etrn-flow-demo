# Audience

## Primary segment

A small or medium company with regular road freight operations, an internal dispatcher or logistics role and no convenient unified workflow around electronic transport documents.

## Typical organizations

- carrier;
- shipper;
- freight forwarder;
- manufacturer;
- distributor;
- wholesale warehouse.

## Decision-maker

- owner;
- head of logistics;
- operations director;
- IT manager;
- chief accountant;
- 1C partner.

## User pain

- information is split between spreadsheets, calls and accounting systems;
- employees do not see the next required action;
- documents remain unfinished;
- managers do not see delays across trips;
- standard operator UI does not reflect internal workflow;
- custom integration appears expensive and unclear.

## Existing alternatives

- manual work in operator UI;
- spreadsheets and messengers;
- custom 1C changes;
- general TMS products;
- bespoke integration by a large integrator.

## Success from user perspective

Within a few minutes the visitor understands how one interface could connect dispatching, driver actions, warehouse stages and document status control.
