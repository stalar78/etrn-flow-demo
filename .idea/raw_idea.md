# Raw Idea

Create a demo application by the same commercial principle as QuoteFlow and ApprovalFlow.

The application will live on a separate StalarVision subdomain and have a dedicated product page on the main site. Targeted advertising can lead to the product page, where a visitor understands the offer and opens the demo.

The demo shows a dispatcher dashboard, shipments, roles, workflow statuses, problems and an integration layer around ETRN. It does not implement legal document exchange in the first release.

The first release should be minimal but respectable. After deployment, development continues based on analytics, commercial response and the needs of a first real pilot customer.
