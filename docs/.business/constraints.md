# Constraints

## Product constraints

- Demonstrate value without pretending to be an accredited operator.
- Use one primary segment first.
- Keep the MVP narrower than a TMS.
- Launch a minimum credible version, then improve after deployment.

## Legal and factual constraints

- Do not claim legal compliance without verification.
- Do not claim integration with an operator before it exists.
- Do not reproduce real signatures or documents.
- Always label synthetic data and demo actions.

## Technical constraints

- Public demo must be safe to reset.
- Visitor-created data must be isolated.
- External integration must be behind an adapter.
- No secrets in frontend or repository.
- Architecture should be practical for one independent developer.

## Commercial constraints

- StalarVision is one independent developer, not a studio.
- No invented clients, reviews, metrics or guarantees.
- The product page must sell individual development and integration, not a non-existent ready legal service.
