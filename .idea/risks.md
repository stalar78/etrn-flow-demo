# Idea Risks

## Why this may fail

1. Customers may prefer standard operator products and not see the need for custom software.
2. Advertising may attract informational rather than commercial traffic.
3. The project may grow into an oversized TMS before launch.
4. A purely visual demo may not prove engineering capability.
5. Incorrect legal language may damage trust.
6. Public guest functionality may be abused.

## Risk reduction

1. Position the product as an integration and workflow layer, not an operator replacement.
2. Use narrow commercial search queries and measure the funnel.
3. Enforce MVP exclusions and stage gates.
4. Implement real state transitions, validation, event history and reset.
5. Keep legal claims conservative and verify them before publication.
6. Use quotas, rate limits, synthetic data and isolated sessions.
