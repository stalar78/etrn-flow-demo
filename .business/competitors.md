# Competitors and alternatives

## Direct large alternatives

Accredited EPD operators and their logistics products solve legally significant document exchange and may provide ready interfaces and integrations.

ETRN Flow must not compete by claiming to replace them.

## Indirect alternatives

- manual operator UI;
- spreadsheets;
- messenger coordination;
- custom 1C modifications;
- general TMS;
- large system integrators.

## Differentiation

- individual interface around the customer's actual workflow;
- clear demonstration before a custom project begins;
- direct work with one developer;
- narrow, staged implementation;
- web and mobile role scenarios;
- integration-ready architecture without forcing a full TMS.

## Research rule

Names, prices, API availability and current operator capabilities must be verified through current official sources before commercial comparison is published.
